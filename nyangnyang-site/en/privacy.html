Meow Shelf Privacy Notice

Effective November 8, 2026 (announced November 1, 2026). Until then, the previous version applies (effective November 4, 2026, or October 29, 2026 before that; see the revision history). However, the book-search and book-information changes in the book database table apply from the day the app update that includes them is released. The random device number and the Google ML Kit diagnostic data for Android text recognition apply from October 25, 2026, together with the Android app launch.

This is the English version of our privacy policy, prepared for users outside Korea. If it conflicts with the Korean version, the Korean version prevails, except where the law of your country of residence requires otherwise.

At a glance

  • What we collect: nothing is required. If you choose to sign in, we receive your email address, an account identifier and a nickname. If you sign in, we also back up your books, quotes, notes and wordbook entries.
  • What we don't do: no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no cookies, no selling of data. Photos are never stored on our servers.
  • Works without an account: adding books, saving quotes and notes, the reading timer and collecting cats all work on your device without signing in.
  • Public feed: only posts you choose to publish are shown to other users, with your nickname. Unpublishing removes them.
  • Your data, your copy: Settings > Back up your records lets you export everything to a file.
  • Deleting your account: removes your account, backup and feed data from our servers immediately. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Email you send us: if you email us, we keep your email address and message to reply and keep a record (3 years after we reply; we use Gmail).
  • Contact: sokurihj@gmail.com

1. Controller and contact

The data controller is NyangBit (“we”, “us”), the individual developer and operator of Meow Shelf (“the app”), based in the Republic of Korea.

Email: sokurihj@gmail.com. The app is run by one person, who handles all privacy requests; we have not appointed a separate Data Protection Officer.

We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. The only thing recorded automatically is on Android: when you ask the app to recognise text, it sends a random device number it generated itself, and our server keeps that number with the day’s request count for three days to limit abuse, then deletes it. It is not linked to your email, nickname, photos or account, is not used for advertising or tracking, and changes if you reinstall the app. Also on Android, when on-device text recognition is used, Google ML Kit does not send the photo or the recognised text, but it may send Google diagnostic and usage data: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name, version), a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you or your device, performance metrics and error codes. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. iOS does neither. How to object: this data is sent only when you use photo text recognition on Android. If you don’t want it sent, type your quote in yourself instead of using recognition; nothing is sent and every other feature works as usual. Any device number already on our server is deleted automatically after three days. We do not process special categories of personal data (Art. 9 GDPR) on purpose. Quotes and notes are free text that you write yourself, so please do not enter sensitive information you don’t want stored.

Camera (to capture passages and scan barcodes), photo library (to pick a book photo) and notification permissions are all optional. If you decline one, only that feature is unavailable.

Camera, photos and text recognition

  • iOS: text in a photo is recognised on your device with Apple Vision. The photo does not leave your device.
  • Android: each time you ask the app to recognise text, the photo is sent through our server function (Supabase) to Google Cloud Vision (Google LLC). We do not store the photo and only return the recognised text to the app. Google states that in this mode it processes the image in memory without writing it to disk and does not use it to train its models. If you are offline, or the daily limit of this service is reached, the app uses Google ML Kit on your device instead, and the photo does not leave your device. Google ML Kit does not send the photo or the recognised text, but it may send Google diagnostic and usage data: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name, version), a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you or your device, performance metrics and error codes. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Android: to limit how many times one device can use text recognition each day, the app also sends a random device number it generated itself with each request. It is not linked to you and changes if you reinstall the app. The server keeps this number and the day’s request count for three days from the day recorded, then deletes them.

Notifications

If you set a target time on the reading timer, the app asks for permission to notify you when time is up. You can decline and the timer still works. Notifications are scheduled on your device only. We use no remote (push) notifications and create or collect no push token.

Paid features

Some features are a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect or store payment details or transaction records; they stay with Apple or Google, and you can see them in your store purchase history.

5. International transfers (Chapter V GDPR)

  • To us (Republic of Korea): we are based in Korea, so data from users in the EEA reaches us there. The European Commission has found that Korea provides an adequate level of data protection (Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/254), so no further safeguard is required.
  • Supabase, Inc. (USA): your data is stored in Seoul, but Supabase staff in the USA may access the servers remotely for operations and fault handling. This transfer relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated in Supabase’s data processing addendum.
  • Google LLC (USA and other countries where Google has data centres): used for Android text recognition, Japanese-language book search, and email you send us (Gmail). This relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated in Google’s data processing terms.
  • Open Library / Internet Archive (USA): receives only the search term or ISBN and your IP address. No account data is sent.

You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards at sokurihj@gmail.com. If you do not want an Android photo to be sent to Google, turn off your internet connection before recognising text; recognition then happens only on your device.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and the right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)) where we rely on consent. You also have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing (Art. 22); we do not make any such decisions.

Most of these you can use inside the app:

  • Change your nickname — Settings
  • Delete individual books, quotes, notes and words
  • Unpublish a feed post — Feed (withdraws consent for that post)
  • Report a post or block a user — Feed
  • Export all your data as a file (JSON text, .nyang) — Settings > Back up your records
  • Delete your account and all server-side data — Settings > Delete account

For anything else, email sokurihj@gmail.com. We reply within one month (extendable by up to two further months for complex requests, in which case we tell you why). Requests are free unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

8. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in the EU/EEA country where you live, work or where you believe an infringement took place. A list is available at edpb.europa.eu. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first, so please contact us as well.

9. Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking technologies. These policy pages are static web pages and set no cookies of their own.

10. Children

Signing in is optional, and without signing in we collect no personal data. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below 16 (or the lower age set by your country, but never below 13) without the consent of the holder of parental responsibility. If you believe a child has signed in without that consent, email us and we will delete the data without delay.

11. Security

  • Your sign-in session is stored on your device encrypted with AES-256, and the key is kept in the system secure storage.
  • All traffic to our server uses HTTPS.
  • Server data is protected by row-level access rules so that you can only read your own data (and the posts other users published).
  • Server access is limited to the operator, and admin credentials are not included in the app.

12. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice we announce it in the app or on this page at least 7 days before the change takes effect, and at least 30 days before if the change significantly affects your rights.
