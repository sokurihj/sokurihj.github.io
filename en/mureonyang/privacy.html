Fetch Cat Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

At a glance
  • The app doesn’t collect personal information. There’s no sign-up or sign-in, and it never asks for your name, email address, or phone number. The app does not communicate with any server of ours.
  • Your saved links stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. Syncing between Mac and iPhone happens through the iCloud of your Apple Account, and we cannot see any of it.
  • The clipboard is read only when you save. When you copy something, the app only checks whether it looks like a web address — it doesn’t read what you copied. It reads the content only when you tap or click a save button.
  • When you save a link, the app fetches its title, icon, and description from that website. Your device connects to the website directly; nothing passes through us.
  • The only personal information we receive is email you send us. If you email us, we keep your email address and message to reply and keep a record (3 years after we reply; we use Gmail).
  • Apple handles tip payments. We never receive your card details or anything that identifies who made a purchase.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools. We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA) or location data.
  • Contact: sokurihj@gmail.com

This policy explains how the developer and operator of the Fetch Cat app for Mac and iPhone ("we") handle information when you use the app. Fetch Cat is operated by an individual developer based in the Republic of Korea.

1. Information on your devices and in your iCloud

The app keeps the following information only on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud. None of it is sent to us, and we cannot see it.

Clipboard — When you copy something, the app only asks the operating system whether it looks like a web address; it does not read what you copied. It reads the content only when you choose to save it (on Mac, by picking a folder in the speech bubble; on iPhone, by tapping the paw button), and it saves only the web address. On Mac, turning off Detect Copied Links or hiding the character stops this check. When you choose “Copy URL,” the app puts that link’s address on your clipboard. Reading the clipboard is optional: you can deny paste access and still save links by typing them in (or, on iPhone, with the Share button). You can change paste access at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security on Mac, or in Settings > Apps > Fetch Cat > Paste from Other Apps on iPhone (menu names vary by OS version).

Titles, icons, and descriptions — When you save a link, your device connects directly to that link’s website to read its title, icon, and description, the same way a web browser opens the page. As with any web visit, the website may see your device’s IP address. For YouTube links, the app sends the link address to YouTube’s public oEmbed lookup (Google LLC) to get the exact title. None of this goes through us, and we do not receive it.

iCloud sync — Links and folders are stored in a private iCloud (CloudKit) database that only you can use. It belongs to your Apple Account, and we cannot access it. Apple Inc. handles iCloud under its terms with you; it is not a service provider of ours. If you aren’t signed in to iCloud or don’t use it, your links stay on that device only.

Your own character images (Mac) — The background of a picture you choose is removed on your Mac using Apple’s on-device image analysis. The picture is never sent to us. The Mac app runs in the App Sandbox and reads only files you pick.

We never receive payment details. Apple processes tips through the App Store on your device; this does not go through any server of ours. We do not receive card or bank information or anything that identifies who made a purchase. Apple keeps the original payment records, which you can see in your Apple Account purchase history.

Crash reports — If you turn on “Share With App Developers” in your device settings, Apple may share app crash and usage statistics (such as device model, OS version, and the app’s state when an error occurred) with us. Apple collects this and provides it in a form that does not identify you, and we use it only to fix bugs. You can turn it off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements on iPhone, or System Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements on Mac.

2. Email you send us

InformationWhyHow long
Your email address, your message (and any attachments), and when you sent itTo reply to you and keep a record of support requests such as purchase or bug issues3 years after we reply, then deleted

We receive and store email with Gmail, provided by Google LLC, so your message is stored on Google’s servers in the United States and other countries. If you’d rather not, simply don’t email us — every feature of the app works without it.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. Apart from Google (Gmail) for email, we do not use service providers that process personal information for us.

When you open a saved link, it opens directly in your web browser on your device and does not pass through us. After that, the website’s own privacy policy applies.

4. Tracking and advertising

The app does not use cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA), analytics tools, or any tracking technology, and does not show ads. We do not make automated decisions that affect your rights.

5. Security

The app does not communicate with any server of ours and does not send your links, folders, or settings to us. When you copy something, it checks only whether it’s a web address, and reads it only when you choose to save. Your iCloud data is visible only through your own Apple Account, and Apple encrypts communication with iCloud. Only the operator can access the support mailbox.

6. Your rights and choices

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your information, or ask us to stop processing it, by emailing sokurihj@gmail.com. The only personal information we hold is email you’ve sent us, so we’ll find the messages from your address and act on them. We do not charge for requests and will respond within 30 days.

Because we cannot access the information on your devices or in your iCloud, you control it directly: delete links and folders in the app (they are removed from iCloud and your other devices too); delete all of the app’s data left in iCloud from Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage on iPhone, or System Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage on Mac (menu names vary by OS version); and remove settings and character images by deleting the app.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or other U.S. states), you may have additional rights under local law. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The app does not ask for information that identifies you. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that an email came from a child under 13, we will use it only to reply and will delete it on a parent’s or guardian’s request.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page at least 7 days before they take effect, and at least 30 days before for significant changes.
