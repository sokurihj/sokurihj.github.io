What it does

On Mac, a cat lives on your desktop. Copy a web address and a speech bubble pops up; pick a folder and the link is saved. On iPhone, tap the paw button to paste, or save straight from the Share button in other apps. Titles, icons and descriptions fill in on their own, and you can add notes, pin favorites and search. Mac and iPhone stay in sync through your own iCloud.

FAQ

Does it read what I copy?

When you copy, the app only checks whether it looks like a web address; it doesn’t read what you copied. It reads the content only when you pick a folder or tap the paw button to save, and it saves only the web address.

Where are my links stored?

On your devices and in your own iCloud. Mac and iPhone sync through the iCloud of your Apple Account. Nothing goes to our servers, so even the developer can’t see them.

How do I save on iPhone?

Tap the paw button to paste a copied link, or use the Share button in Safari, Chrome and other apps.

Do I need to tip to use it?

No. Everything is free. Giving the cat a treat is optional.
