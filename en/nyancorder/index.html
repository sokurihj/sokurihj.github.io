What it does

Tap the shutter for a photo, hold it for a video. Swipe through 7 filters: Home Video, B&W, Disposable, Digicam, Film, Night Shot and Glitch. Your shots are saved to the Photos app and can go straight to Instagram Stories. Turn your phone sideways to shoot in landscape.

FAQ

Is it free?

The Home Video and B&W filters are free. You can try the other five too, but a logo is added to your photos and videos until you buy them. One purchase unlocks all seven without the logo, for good.

How do I take photos and videos?

Tap the shutter for a photo, or hold it to record a video. Turn your phone sideways to shoot in landscape.

Where are my shots saved?

In the Photos app on your iPhone. Filters are applied on your device, and nothing is sent to a server.

Can I post straight to Instagram Stories?

Yes. After you shoot, tap the Instagram Story button and it opens right in Instagram.
