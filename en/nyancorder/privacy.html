Nyancorder Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · Last updated October 22, 2026

At a glance
  • The app doesn’t collect personal information. There’s no sign-up or sign-in, and it never asks for your name, email address, or phone number. The app does not communicate with any server of ours.
  • Your photos and videos are processed only on your device. Filters are applied on the device, and the results are saved to your Photos app. Nothing is sent to us.
  • The only personal information we receive is email you send us. If you email us, we keep your email address and message to reply and keep a record (3 years after we reply; we use Gmail).
  • Sharing happens only when you tap a button. When you tap Share or Instagram Story, the file goes directly from your device to the app you choose.
  • Apple handles payments. We never receive your card details or anything that identifies who made a purchase.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools. We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA) or location data.
  • Contact: sokurihj@gmail.com

This policy explains how the developer and operator of the Nyancorder app ("we") handle information when you use the app. Nyancorder is operated by an individual developer based in the Republic of Korea.

1. Information on your device

The app uses your device’s features only on the device, for these purposes, and keeps the following information only on your device. None of it is sent to us, and we cannot see it.

InformationWhere it is keptWhy
Photos and videos you take (with the filter and date/time text applied; videos include sound)Your Photos appThe camera is used to take photos and videos with a camcorder-style filter, and the microphone to record sound for videos. The app asks only for permission to add to your photo library and cannot read your other photos.
The video you just recordedThe app’s temporary folder, until your next captureSo you can play it back in the full-screen viewer
App settings (the last filter you chose, whether to keep showing the video-recording tip)The app’s storage on your device, until you delete the appTo pick up where you left off
A photo or video you send to Instagram StoriesYour device’s clipboard, deleted automatically after 5 minutes (immediately if Instagram can’t be opened)To hand the file to Instagram

Camera access is required to take pictures. Microphone and photo library access are optional: without the microphone, videos have no sound; without photo access, your captures won’t be saved to the Photos app.

Date and time — Some filters stamp the date and time from your device’s clock onto the photo or video. The app does not use location data.

We never receive payment details. Apple processes the “Remove Logo” purchase, and the app checks on the device whether you’ve bought it using the purchase record Apple keeps there. We do not receive card or bank information or anything that identifies who made a purchase. Apple keeps the original payment records, which you can see in your Apple Account purchase history.

Crash reports — If you turn on “Share With App Developers” in your device settings, Apple may share app crash and usage statistics (such as device model, OS version, and the app’s state when an error occurred) with us. Apple collects this and provides it in a form that does not identify you, and we use it only to fix bugs. You can turn it off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

2. Email you send us

InformationWhyHow long
Your email address, your message (and any attachments), and when you sent itTo reply to you and keep a record of support requests such as purchase or bug issues3 years after we reply, then deleted

We receive and store email with Gmail, provided by Google LLC, so your message is stored on Google’s servers in the United States and other countries. If you’d rather not, simply don’t email us — every feature of the app works without it.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. Apart from Google (Gmail) for email, we do not use service providers that process personal information for us.

When you share a photo or video with the app’s share button or to Instagram Stories, it goes directly from your device to the app you choose; it does not pass through us. After that, the receiving app’s privacy policy applies (for Instagram, that of Meta Platforms, Inc.). When sending to Instagram Stories, the app also passes along a public app number issued by Meta; it identifies the app, not you.

4. Tracking and advertising

The app does not use cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA), analytics tools, or any tracking technology, and does not show ads. We do not make automated decisions that affect your rights.

5. Security

The app does not communicate with any server of ours and does not send photos, videos, or settings off your device. It asks only for permission to add to your photo library, so it cannot read your other photos. Only the operator can access the support mailbox. Files placed on the clipboard for Instagram Stories are deleted after 5 minutes, and the temporary video file is deleted at your next capture.

6. Your rights and choices

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your information, or ask us to stop processing it, by emailing sokurihj@gmail.com. The only personal information we hold is email you’ve sent us, so we’ll find the messages from your address and act on them. We do not charge for requests and will respond within 30 days.

You can delete photos and videos in the Photos app, and remove the app’s settings and temporary files by deleting the app. You can turn off camera, microphone, and photo library access at any time in Settings > Nyancorder.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or other U.S. states), you may have additional rights under local law. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The app does not ask for information that identifies you. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that an email came from a child under 13, we will use it only to reply and will delete it on a parent’s or guardian’s request.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page at least 7 days before they take effect, and at least 30 days before for significant changes.
