Book passage log (Korean only)

Found a line worth keeping? Snap the page and the passage is saved as text.

The app is currently available in Korean only.

Don’t let great lines get lost in your camera roll, meow

What it does

Open your book and take a photo — the passage is recognized and written out for you. Sort your shelf into to-read, reading and finished, and keep notes, a word list and even a character map for each book. Every book you add brings one more cat to your cat tower.
