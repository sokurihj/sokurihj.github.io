    Planning, drawing, coding and answering your emails — all done by one person. Your message is read by the person who made the app.

  2. A minute a day

    Not apps that hold on to you, but apps you open for a moment once a day and close again.

  3. A cat in every app

    Every app has a cat living in it, so keeping a record on your own feels a little less lonely.
