Photograph animals and plants and AI tells you what they are, then cuts out the star of the shot as a sticker for your album. FAQ

Do I need to log in?

No. No sign-up, no account. Install it and start snapping and collecting.

How many photos can I take for free?

You can identify 3 photos free every day, and the free count resets at midnight. Want more? Buy film — one film identifies one photo. Photos with no animal or plant, or ones the AI isn’t sure about, don’t use up your count.

What happens to my film if I switch phones?

Film is tied to a number kept in your iPhone’s Keychain, so it stays if you reinstall the app on the same iPhone. It doesn’t move to another phone automatically. To move your remaining film, email us with the purchase date and product.

Where do my photos go?

To identify animals and plants, only the photo inside the viewfinder frame is sent through our server to an external AI service (Google). Our server doesn’t store photos. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where are my location and walks stored?

The places you meet friends and the routes you walk are stored only on your phone and never sent to a server. Before deleting the app or switching phones, make a file with Settings → Backup and import it on your new phone.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If film didn’t arrive after a purchase, please email us.
