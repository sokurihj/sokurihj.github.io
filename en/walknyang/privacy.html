WalkNyang Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

At a glance
  • No sign-in. The app never asks for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your location and walks stay on your device. Where you met each friend, the routes you walked, and your album photos are never sent to us.
  • Photos go to an AI service only to identify animals and plants. The part of the photo inside the viewfinder frame is sent through our server to an external AI service (Google LLC, USA). Our server does not store photos, and the app asks for your consent before sending a photo for the first time.
  • Our server keeps only a random device number and its ledger: how many photos you took today and how many films you bought and used.
  • Apple processes payments. RevenueCat keeps the film purchase records; we never receive card details.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Questions or requests: sokurihj@gmail.com

1. Who we are

WalkNyang (the "app") is developed and operated by an individual developer in the Republic of Korea under the name NyangBit ("we"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: sokurihj@gmail.com.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as we are not required to. We have not appointed a representative in the European Union or the United Kingdom under Article 27(2) GDPR, because our processing is limited, does not include special categories of data, and is unlikely to result in a risk to your rights. You can reach us directly by email in any language.

Providing this information is not a legal requirement. Without the photo we cannot identify what you photographed, and without the device number we cannot keep track of free photos and films; the rest of the app still works.

The public key used for app verification is not stored together with your device number or photos and cannot identify you on its own. We do not process special categories of personal data.

Please photograph only the animal or plant. If a person's face or other personal information appears inside the frame, it will be sent to the AI service as is.

Permissions

Camera (to photograph animals and plants), location while using the app (to record where you met each friend and the route you walk on your device; during a walk it keeps recording with the screen off, and iPhone shows a location indicator), and adding to Photos (to save a share image when you choose "Save Image") are all optional. Without them, only the related feature is unavailable. You can change permissions in your device settings at any time.

Information that stays on your device

Your album (photos, cutouts, names and descriptions), where and when you met each friend, your walks (location coordinates, distance, time), the map cells you have walked, your settings, and your AI consent choice are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. A backup file you create under "Backup" in Settings is saved where you choose (such as the Files app or iCloud Drive) and does not pass through us.

5. International transfers

We operate from the Republic of Korea, which the European Commission and the United Kingdom recognize as providing adequate protection (adequacy decisions). Our providers process data in the United States and other countries. For transfers to them we rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the Standard Contractual Clauses included in the provider's data processing terms. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards by email.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict how we process it;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing — the AI only names the animal or plant in your photo, and we make no decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you;
  • withdraw your consent at any time — simply stop using photo identification; deleting the app also resets your choice. Withdrawing does not affect processing that already took place.

To make a request, email sokurihj@gmail.com. Because there is no sign-in, we don't know who you are; we'll explain how to identify your ledger (for example, the date and product of a film purchase). For email you've sent us, we'll find the messages from your address. Requests are free, and we respond within one month. Information stored only on your device can be deleted from the album detail screen ("Delete from album") or by deleting the app.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. You may also contact the Personal Information Protection Commission of Korea.

California and other U.S. states

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information. The app does not track you across apps or websites, so it does not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law; you can use them by emailing us, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

7. Tracking and advertising

The app does not use cookies, the advertising identifier (IDFA), analytics tools, or any tracking technology, and does not show ads.

8. Security

All communication between the app, our server, and our providers is encrypted with HTTPS. Only the operator can access the server, the database, and the support mailbox, and service access keys are kept in the server's secret storage, never in the app. Apple App Attest and per-address rate limits protect the service from abuse. We send only the part of the photo inside the frame, keep as little as possible, and delete expired records automatically.

9. Children

The app does not ask for information that identifies you. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 (under 13 in the United States). If we learn that an email came from such a child, we will use it only to reply. A parent or guardian may contact us to access or delete records related to their child.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page at least 7 days before they take effect, and at least 30 days before for significant changes.
