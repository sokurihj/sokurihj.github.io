MoaNyang Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026 (announced October 22, 2026) · First published October 7, 2026

At a glance
  • No sign-in. The app never asks for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your records stay on your device. Your savings, daily log, wish list, pixel art, and settings are never sent to our server.
  • Photos are sent to an AI service only to turn them into pixel art. Our server does not store photos, the AI service (fal.ai) is set not to keep a record of the request, and the generated image is deleted within one hour.
  • Our server keeps only three things: a random device identifier, how many films you have left, and — if you buy films — the Apple transaction number or Google Play purchase token and product.
  • No ads, no tracking, no cookies. We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android).
  • Email you send us: if you email us, we keep your email address and message to reply and keep a record (3 years after we reply; we use Gmail).
  • Questions or deletion requests: sokurihj@gmail.com

This policy explains how the developer and operator of the MoaNyang app ("we") handle information when you use the app. MoaNyang is operated by an individual developer based in the Republic of Korea.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhenWhy

Camera access (to photograph items) and photo library access (to save a share card when you choose “Save Image”) are optional. Without the camera you can’t add items by photo, but everything else keeps working.

We never receive payment details. Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) processes all payments; we do not collect or store card or bank information.

Please photograph only the item. If a person’s face or other personal information appears in a photo, it will be sent to the conversion service as is.

Information that stays on your device — your savings, daily log, item names, prices and pixel art, coins, accessories, and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

2. How long we keep it

Expired records are deleted automatically every day. On iPhone, the identifier is kept in the Keychain, so it survives deleting and reinstalling the app, and your remaining films carry over. The identifier in the Keychain is removed when you erase the iPhone.

On Android, the identifier also stays the same when you reinstall the app, so your films carry over. However, it changes if you factory-reset the device or move to a new one, and films left on the old device cannot be carried over.

If you stop using the app, the server record is deleted on the schedule above.

3. Service providers

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. We use these service providers, located in the United States and other countries, to run the app:

ProviderPurposeLocation
fal.aiAI conversion of photos into pixel artUnited States
Cloudflare, Inc.Server hosting (relaying conversion requests, storing the film ledger and purchase records)United States and other Cloudflare data center locations
Google LLCReceiving and storing email you send us (Gmail)United States and other Google data center locations

To verify purchases, we look up the transaction number on Apple’s App Store server (Apple Inc., United States) for iPhone, or the purchase token on the Google Play server (Google LLC, United States) for Android. Apple and Google process payments under their own agreements with you and are not our processors. They hold the original payment records, which you can see in your Apple Account purchase history or your Google Play order history.

Before the app sends a photo for the first time, it tells you that photos are sent to an external AI service and asks for your consent. If you decline, you can’t add new items by photo, but everything else — logging, the calendar, and the shop — keeps working.

When you share an image with the app’s share button or to Instagram Stories, it goes directly from your device to the app you choose; it does not pass through us. You decide each time whether to include an amount in the image.

4. Tracking and advertising

The app does not use cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), analytics tools, or any tracking technology, and does not show ads.

5. Security

All communication between the app, our server, and our providers is encrypted with HTTPS. Only the operator can access the server, the database, and the support mailbox, and service access keys are kept in the server’s secret storage, never in the app. We keep as little as possible and delete expired records automatically.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your information, or ask us to stop processing it, by emailing sokurihj@gmail.com. Because there is no sign-in, we don’t know who you are; when you contact us we’ll explain how to identify your record (for example, the date and product of a purchase). For email you’ve sent us, we’ll find the messages from your address. We do not charge for requests and will respond within 30 days. Information stored only on your device can be removed by deleting the app.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or other U.S. states), you may have additional rights under local law. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. The AI is used only to redraw your photo as pixel art; we do not make automated decisions that affect your rights.

7. Children

The app does not ask for information that identifies you and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that an email came from a child under 13, we will use it only to reply. A parent or guardian may contact us to access or delete records related to their child.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page at least 7 days before they take effect, and at least 30 days before for significant changes.
